Is Sellwood Middle School headed below 400 students?

Portland Public Schools says Sellwood's enrollment will drop below 400, and the School Board is weighing closures against projections for the 2031-32 school year. This page compares that projection with Oregon's official enrollment counts and Portland State University's past forecasts.

Data through fall 2025-26 · Updated October 2026

Bottom line

  • Sellwood has 566 students today, and enrollment has stayed between 550 and 613 since 2015-16.
  • Most evidence points to Sellwood falling below 400 by 2031-32, the year the School Board is using. PSU projects 350. Our own model projects about 356 to 408, depending on assumptions.
  • Staying above 400 would require Sellwood to keep enrolling feeder students at about its current record share (97%).
  • PSU's longer-range forecast shows a partial rebound to about 380 by 2035-36, still below 400. PSU bases those later years on its own kindergarten and population assumptions, not on children already enrolled.
  • The driver is smaller elementary classes: enrollment at the three feeder schools has fallen 31% since 2015-16.
  • Single-school forecasts are uncertain. PSU's forecasts for Sellwood's 2025-26 enrollment ranged from 10% too low to on target, and PSU publishes no error range for individual schools.
  • PSU also projects Hosford (374) and Brentwood/Lane (357), the likely receiving schools, below 400 in 2031-32.

If Sellwood closes: can Hosford and Brentwood hold its students?

Sellwood would close in fall 2028. PPS's published grade 6-8 boundary maps (October 2026) show how Sellwood's area would be split:

  • Scenario A: everything west of SE McLoughlin Blvd goes to Hosford. That is the Llewellyn area plus the part of the Duniway area south of SE Tacoma St. Everything east of McLoughlin (the rest of the Duniway area and the Lewis area) goes to Brentwood.
  • Scenario B: only the Llewellyn area (west of McLoughlin, north of Tacoma) goes to Hosford. The whole Duniway area, including Sellwood south of Tacoma, and the Lewis area go to Brentwood.
  • Both scenarios: Hosford takes Sunnyside's middle grades, and Hosford's area east of SE Cesar Chavez Blvd (the Creston Elementary area) moves to Kellogg.

How much weight can a single-school forecast bear?

PPS describes its projections as PSU’s “medium scenario.” That label refers to PSU’s district-wide forecast; for individual schools, PSU publishes only this one scenario, with no error range. For Sellwood’s 2025-26 enrollment, PSU’s forecasts ranged from 10% too low to on target, an average of about 5% too low. The July 2026 forecast of 350 for 2031-32 is PSU’s lowest yet, partly because each new forecast adds another year of smaller elementary classes. Even a 5% to 10% correction would leave Sellwood below 400 (about 368 to 389). A published range for each affected school would show the Board how likely different outcomes are.

Method. Feeders are Lewis, Duniway and Llewellyn. Each year's 6th grade = the prior year's feeder 5th grade × the enrollment share you choose. Elementary classes advance at the feeders' average grade-to-grade ratios for 2022-23 to 2025-26. 7th and 8th grades carry forward at the retention rate you choose. The model assumes current boundaries and no new programs; it is a check on PSU's forecast, not a replacement for it.
